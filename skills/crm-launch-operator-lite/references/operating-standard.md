# Owned, Moving, Trusted

Use this standard to keep a CRM launch grounded in operating behavior rather than features.

## Owned

Every active opportunity has one accountable owner and one visible next action. If ownership can be shared or blank, the owner needs a simple fallback rule before launch.

## Moving

Stages describe observable progress in the customer's buying process. A deal moves because evidence changed, not because time passed or a seller wanted a cleaner board.

## Trusted

The data is complete and consistent enough to support a named recurring management decision. Trust does not require every possible field; it requires the few inputs behind the decision to be reliably maintained.

## How to use the standard

For Lite, identify the clearest strength and the most important gap. Do not turn the check into full system design. Recommend the smallest next decision that would make the launch safer or simpler.
