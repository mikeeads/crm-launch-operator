# Privacy

CRM Launch Operator Lite is instruction-only. The repository and skill make no network requests,
do not collect analytics, and do not send your CRM data anywhere. Any answers or files created by
the skill stay in the workspace where you run it unless you choose to share them.

The AI host you use may process prompts and files under its own terms and settings. Review that
provider's privacy controls before adding business information, and use sanitized examples when
you can. For the CRM Coach website privacy policy, see [crm.coach/privacy](https://crm.coach/privacy).
